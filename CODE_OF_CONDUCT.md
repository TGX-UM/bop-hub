# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to each other. Criticise pathways, evidence and code, not the people who
contributed them.

## Scope

This applies to everything in and around this repository: issues, pull requests, code
review, commit messages and any BOP-Hub space elsewhere.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume a contributor is acting in good faith, and that a disagreement about evidence is
  a disagreement about evidence.
- Keep review about the work. "This rating overstates what the cited study shows" is
  useful; "you don't understand the literature" is not.
- Accept that people's expertise and their first language differ. Someone who knows the
  biology may not know Jekyll, and someone who knows Jekyll may not know the biology.
  Both are contributing.
- Give credit. If a pathway came out of someone's reading, name them as curator.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, sustained disruption, publishing
others' private information, and sexual attention or imagery. Also, specific to a
resource like this one: knowingly misrepresenting evidence, inflating an evidence grade
to make an intervention look better supported than it is, or using BOP-Hub to market a
product or promote a health claim.

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainers by email, or open an issue if it can be discussed in
public. Reports go to Marvin Martens at the Department of Translational Genomics,
Maastricht University; the address is on the
[department's staff pages](https://www.maastrichtuniversity.nl/research/translational-genomics).
If a report concerns a maintainer, raise it with the department instead.

Reports are handled confidentially. We will acknowledge receipt, look into what happened,
and say what we decided. Responses range from a private word to reverting a contribution
to a permanent ban from the project.
