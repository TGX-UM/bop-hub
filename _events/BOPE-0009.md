Recorded as a distinct event because it is the mechanistic bridge to the antioxidant branch, and because its interpretation is double-edged: raised urate is proposed here as protective, while in other contexts it is a risk factor. A curator extending this BOP should look at where this event appears in adverse pathways too.

A related GO term, `GO:0015747` (urate transport), describes the renal mechanism; `GO:0046415` (urate metabolic process) is used in the `process:` slot as the broader, directionally neutral class.
