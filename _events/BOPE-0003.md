A 36-hour fast in human skeletal muscle clearly modulated AMPK-mTOR signalling and several autophagy mediators, whereas shorter fasting periods produced only modest and ambiguous changes in autophagy markers. This is the best direct human evidence in the source for the step, and it is why the quantitative understanding of the downstream relationship is rated low: the threshold fasting duration in humans is not established.
