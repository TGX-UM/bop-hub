In MASLD, excess hepatic lipid storage promotes oxidative stress by activating mitochondria, the endoplasmic reticulum and NADPH oxidase. The resulting reactive oxygen species interfere with insulin signalling and reshape lipid-handling enzyme regulation, sustaining steatosis. Reducing oxidative stress therefore feeds back into insulin sensitivity.

Supporting evidence is indirect: experimental suppression of urate production with allopurinol has been associated with poorer physical performance and higher oxidative stress. The GO term is an unsigned `regulation of` class, which is acceptable because the direction is carried by `action:`.
