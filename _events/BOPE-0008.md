In humans, plasma ketones begin to rise within 24 hours of fasting. Beta-hydroxybutyrate is not only a fuel: it acts as a signalling metabolite, and this event is therefore the hub from which the anti-inflammatory and antioxidant branches of the pathway both depart.

Mitochondrial dysfunction in cirrhosis can blunt ketogenesis despite lipolysis, and uncontrolled type 1 diabetes causes unrestrained ketone accumulation and ketoacidosis. Both bound the applicability of this event, and of the BOP as a whole.
