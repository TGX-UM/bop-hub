This is the most directly evidenced lipid-clearance step in the pathway. In hepatocytes and mouse liver, autophagy inhibition increased triglyceride content roughly two-fold, demonstrating direct lipid clearance via autophagic flux.

Lipophagy is modelled as its own event rather than being folded into autophagy, because it is the branch that acts on the outcome directly and carries its own evidence.
