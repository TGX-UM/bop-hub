Once active, AMPK phosphorylates key enzymes to suppress energy-consuming processes and promote energy-producing ones. It inactivates acetyl-CoA carboxylase, lowering malonyl-CoA, which relieves inhibition of mitochondrial fatty acid import and so stimulates beta-oxidation.

### No Gene Ontology process term

The `process:` slot is deliberately empty. The closest candidate, `GO:0061762` (CAMKK-AMPK signaling cascade), describes the calcium/calmodulin route specifically, whereas the fasting response works through allosteric AMP binding and LKB1. No unsigned GO term covers AMPK activation as such, and an inaccurate term is worse than none. The protein is recorded in the `object:` slot instead and the direction in `action:`.

### Essentiality

Pharmacological AMPK activation can normalise hepatic triglyceride content on its own, and AMPK inhibition is associated with chemically induced hepatic fat accumulation. The paper treats this event as a prerequisite for lipid clearance.
