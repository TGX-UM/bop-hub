Two inputs converge here: substrates liberated by lipophagy, and AMPK/PPARalpha co-activation. A 5:2 intermittent fasting regimen prevented MASH development, ameliorated established fibrosis and blunted the transition to hepatocellular carcinoma through cooperative PPARalpha- and PCK1-mediated pathways, which the authors read as hepatoprotection partly independent of caloric restriction.

In MASLD and MASH, mitochondrial dysfunction and hepatic insulin resistance may blunt this event, particularly at advanced disease stages. PPARalpha-deficient mice show hypoketonaemia and a markedly blunted transcriptional response to fasting.
