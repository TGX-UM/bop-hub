Two routes converge. The energy deficit lowers systemic CCL2 signalling via AMPK, reducing monocyte release from the bone marrow. Independently, beta-hydroxybutyrate has been reported to inhibit NLRP3 inflammasome-mediated IL-1beta and IL-18 production in monocytes and macrophages.

Intermittent fasting studies suggest lower baseline circulating proinflammatory cytokines. In a mouse model, intermittent fasting improved liver fat and insulin resistance with the benefit linked to macrophage migration inhibitory factor, an immunoregulatory cytokine with characterised antifibrotic effects. The source stresses that the immune effect occurs "without impairing emergency immune responses", which matters for the safety framing of any beneficial pathway.
