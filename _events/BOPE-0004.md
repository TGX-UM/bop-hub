Glucocorticoid-dependent induction of KLF15 together with downregulation of SREBP-1c reinforces the suppression. Chronic energy surplus and hepatic de novo lipogenesis are among the combined causes of MASLD, so removing this drive addresses the disease at its origin rather than only its consequences.
