This is the initiating event of every fasting BOP. Mesnage and Buchinger define it as "the acute energy deficit created by the withdrawal of external nutrient supply", and propose a canonical form for classification: substantial acute caloric withdrawal of roughly 500 kcal/day or less, or complete caloric abstinence for over 24 hours.

### How it is measured

The paper argues that ketosis is the most reliable and clinically practical biomarker of activation, because it marks the transition to a catabolic state and can be read from urine with a nitroprusside strip. Ketonaemia becomes detectable after about 21 hours of water-only fasting without exercise and about 17.5 hours with exercise, and ketonuria correlates well with blood beta-hydroxybutyrate.

### Why fasting itself is not the event

Fasting is the intervention, recorded in the BOP's `interventions:` slot, not an event. Different protocols engage this event to different degrees: a water-only fast depletes glycogen and reaches ketosis within 24-48 hours, whereas time-restricted eating without caloric reduction may not produce a sufficient deficit at all. The TREATY-FLD trial found that 8-hour time-restricted eating gave no liver-fat reduction beyond caloric restriction, which the authors read as benefit mediated by weight loss rather than by fasting as such.
