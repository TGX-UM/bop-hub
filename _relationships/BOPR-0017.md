Improved insulin sensitivity sustains hepatic fat reduction. Insulin resistance is one of the constitutive causes of MASLD, so reversing it addresses the disease mechanism directly.

The source describes this as closing a positive feedback loop that consolidates the beneficial outcome. The reverse edge is deliberately not recorded, because the acyclic grammar cannot carry it; see the discussion on the BOP page.
