Falling ATP raises the AMP/ATP and ADP/ATP ratios, which allosterically activate AMPK. This is textbook cellular energy sensing and the best-understood step in the pathway. Quantitative understanding is rated moderate rather than high because the ratio thresholds that produce a given degree of activation in human liver in vivo are not well characterised.
