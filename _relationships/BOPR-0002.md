AMPK inhibits mTORC1 both directly and through TSC2. Empirical support in the fasting context comes from human skeletal muscle, where a 36-hour fast clearly modulated AMPK-mTOR signalling; shorter fasts produced only modest and ambiguous changes. Quantitative understanding is low: the fasting duration needed to reach a given degree of mTORC1 inhibition in human liver is not established.
