Fasting reduces the insulin and nutrient signals that sustain SREBP-1c-mediated de novo lipogenesis, and AMPK activation suppresses the lipogenic programme. Glucocorticoid-dependent induction of KLF15 with downregulation of SREBP-1c reinforces the suppression.
