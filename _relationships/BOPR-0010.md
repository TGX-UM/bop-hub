Beta-hydroxybutyrate acts as a signalling metabolite, with reported inhibition of NLRP3 inflammasome-mediated IL-1beta and IL-18 production in monocytes and macrophages. Intermittent fasting studies suggest lower baseline circulating proinflammatory cytokines. Empirical support is moderate: the inflammasome work is largely ex vivo and in model systems rather than in human liver.
