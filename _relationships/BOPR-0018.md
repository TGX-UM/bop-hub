The transition from steatosis to steatohepatitis is the turning point toward progressive disease, and fibrosis severity rises with inflammation and hepatocellular injury, so lowering inflammation should act on progression. In experimental steatohepatitis a 5:2 regimen improved established inflammation and fibrosis and reduced progression toward liver cancer via AMPK/PPARalpha co-activation. Clinical results in steatohepatitis are more heterogeneous, with fasting effective against fibrosis only in obese patients, which caps empirical support at moderate.
