The empirical shortcut from initiating event to outcome, recorded as non-adjacent because it spans the whole pathway. It carries the clinical weight of the BOP: a meta-analysis of 21 studies and 1226 participants found fasting decreased liver fat and steatosis independently of participant age, health status, weight status or intervention duration.

Quantitative understanding is moderate because a dose-response exists: in an observational study of periodic fasting with a mean duration of 8.5 days, the reduction in fatty liver index showed a modest but significant relationship with fasting duration and tracked the magnitude of BMI reduction. Fasting dose is defined by the depth of the energy deficit and its repetition over time, not duration alone.
