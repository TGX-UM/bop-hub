Non-adjacent: the source routes this through immunometabolic signalling. The energy deficit lowers systemic CCL2 signalling via AMPK activation, which reduces monocyte release from the bone marrow and so decreases inflammatory immune tone, without impairing emergency immune responses. Biological plausibility is rated moderate rather than high because the intervening immunometabolic events are described rather than individually evidenced in the liver.
