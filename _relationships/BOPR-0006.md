AMPK inactivates acetyl-CoA carboxylase, which lowers malonyl-CoA and so relieves inhibition of carnitine palmitoyltransferase-mediated mitochondrial fatty acid import, stimulating beta-oxidation. The malonyl-CoA node makes this one of the few steps where a quantitative chain of intermediates is actually measurable, hence moderate rather than low quantitative understanding.
