Lipophagy is the selective autophagy of lipid droplets, so it depends on general autophagic competence. Empirical support is rated moderate: the hepatocyte and mouse liver experiments show that blocking autophagy raises triglyceride content, which is strong evidence for the lipid-clearing function, but the quantitative coupling between bulk autophagic flux and lipid droplet turnover is not resolved.
