Acetyl-CoA generated by beta-oxidation exceeding tricarboxylic acid cycle capacity is routed to ketone body synthesis. In humans plasma ketones begin to rise within 24 hours of fasting, and ketonaemia is detectable from about 21 hours without exercise and 17.5 hours with exercise, which gives this relationship an unusually concrete time course.
