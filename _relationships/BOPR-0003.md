mTORC1 restrains autophagy initiation, so its inhibition releases the autophagic machinery. Biologically this is as well established as anything in the pathway. Empirical support in fasting humans rests largely on autophagy mediator measurements rather than on flux, and quantitative understanding is low because autophagy is hard to quantify in humans at all.
