Reactive oxygen species interfere with insulin signalling, so reducing oxidative stress improves insulin sensitivity. This is a well-described mechanism in general metabolic physiology; its specific contribution within fasting-induced MASLD resolution, relative to the much larger lipid-mobilisation route, is not quantified.
