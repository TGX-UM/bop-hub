Urate is a strong antioxidant in plasma, and extended fasting raises antioxidant capacity while lowering lipid peroxidation. Empirical support is rated **low**: the supporting evidence is the indirect observation that suppressing urate production with allopurinol worsens physical performance and oxidative stress. No study in the source demonstrates that the fasting-induced urate rise is what lowers hepatic oxidative stress. This is the weakest link in the BOP and the obvious target for a dedicated study.
